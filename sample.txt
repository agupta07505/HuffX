Every bit counts. That is the entire philosophy of this project.

Huffman coding gives every frequent symbol a short binary code and every rare symbol a long one, so the average length of the file quietly melts toward its entropy. The recipe never changes: count the symbols, merge the two lightest nodes again and again, then walk the tree writing a zero for every left edge and a one for every right edge.
